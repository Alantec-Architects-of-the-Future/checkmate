# 🛡️ Code of Conduct & Technical Governance

## Our Commitment

In the interest of fostering an open, professional, and deterministic engineering environment, we as contributors and maintainers of the **checkmate** ecosystem commit to making participation a harassment-free experience for everyone.

---

## 📐 Technical & Behavioral Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our project include:

* **Demonstrating Empathy & Respect:** Interacting professionally with all community members.
* **Technical Integrity:** Providing clear, verifiable code, actionable reproduction steps, and structured feedback.
* **Architectural Consistency:** Adhering to zero-dependency design principles and strict schema validation standards.
* **Accepting Constructive Criticism:** Gracefully giving and receiving professional engineering feedback.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include trolling, derogatory comments, personal attacks, and any conduct considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## ⚖️ Anti-Gaming & Quality Assurance Policy

To protect the integrity of the **checkmate** knowledge base and the GitHub Discussions framework:

1. **Organic Solutions Only:** Marking answers in Q&A via collusion, alternate accounts, or spamming non-functional responses is strictly prohibited.
2. **Maintainer Authority:** Repository maintainers reserve the absolute right to unmark, hide, or delete responses that fail to provide real technical value or exhibit gaming behavior.
3. **Badge Integrity:** Any manipulation targeting GitHub achievements (e.g., *Galaxy Brain*) will result in response retraction and platform moderation flags.

---

## 🛠️ Enforcement & Governance

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.
